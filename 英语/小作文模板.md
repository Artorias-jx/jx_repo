## 一、四大底盘总览与适用题材

所有考研英语一小作文可归入四个核心底盘，再搭配中间段“九宫格功能句”拼装，全文稳定控制在 **8—9 句、100—115 词**。

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|**底盘类型**|**覆盖具体题材**|**语域与格式核心**|
|**底盘一：主动信**|邀请信、主动建议信、感谢信、道歉信、投诉信、推荐信、申请信、辞职信|称呼（统一用逗号）+ 署名（无句号）；正式/半正式用 `Yours sincerely,`，同学熟人用 `Yours,`|
|**底盘二：被动回复信**|答复求助/提建议、答复咨询、答复邀请（接受/婉拒）、答复投诉|开头必须先回应来信并**完整复述对方主题**，结尾呼应前文具体事项|
|**底盘三：通知（Notice）**|招募类通知、活动/讲座类通知|居中标题 `Notice` + 右下署名与日期；语气正式客观，**不用感叹号**|
|**底盘四：纪要（Minutes）**|会议纪要、活动讨论纪要|顶部元信息块（标题/日期/地点/参会人/记录人）；**第三人称过去时，无称呼与落款**|

## 二、四大底盘标准模板（首段 + 尾段 + 格式）

### 1. 底盘一：主动信

> **称呼**：`Dear [Name / Professor / Sir or Madam],`（致信教授或英式语境统一用**逗号**，不用冒号）

**首段（按题材选用，务必带齐题干全部限定词）**：

- **正式邀请信（学术/竞赛/讲座，注意逻辑主语一致）**：
    
    - _个人身份视角_：`As a member of [组织，如 the Student Union], I am honored to invite you to [具体动作，如 organize a team for / deliver a lecture at] [活动全称] to be held at our university.`
        
    - _集体代表视角_：`On behalf of [组织], it is my great honor to invite you to [具体动作] [活动全称] to be held at [地点].`
        
    - _通用简洁版_：`I am writing to sincerely invite you to attend / deliver a lecture on [完整主题].`
        
- **主动建议信**：`I am writing to offer several practical suggestions on how to improve [完整主题].`
    
- **感谢信**：`I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for your generous help with [具体事项].`
    
- **道歉信**：`I am writing to offer my sincere apologies for [具体过失，如 failing to attend...].`
    
- **投诉信**：`I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with [产品/服务名称], which I purchased on [日期].`
    
- **推荐信**：`I am writing to strongly recommend [具体人/书/电影/地点] to you for [具体用途/活动].`
    
- **申请信**：`I am writing to apply for the position of [岗位/项目名称] advertised on [渠道，如 the university website].`
    
- **辞职信**：`I am writing to formally inform you of my decision to resign from my position as [职位] effective [离职日期].`
    

**尾段（按题材与对象匹配收尾）**：

- **邀请信（致教授/专家，避免催促感）**：`We truly hope you will accept this invitation and contribute your invaluable guidance（或 share your insightful perspectives）. I would be grateful if you could let us know your decision at your earliest convenience.`
    
- **邀请信（致同辈/熟人）**：`We sincerely hope you will join us and make this event a success. Please let us know your decision by [日期].`
    
- **建议信**：`I hope these suggestions will be helpful to you. Please feel free to contact me if you need further details.`
    
- **感谢信**：`Thank you again for your kindness, and I hope to have the chance to return the favor soon.`
    
- **道歉信**：`Once again, I am terribly sorry for any inconvenience caused, and I hope you can accept my apology.`
    
- **投诉信**：`I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter promptly and offer a satisfactory solution.`
    
- **推荐信**：`I truly hope you will find [it / him / her] helpful. I look forward to hearing your thoughts.`
    
- **申请信**：`I have attached my resume for your review. I would be grateful if you could grant me an interview at your earliest convenience.`
    
- **辞职信**：`I sincerely appreciate the support and opportunities you have provided during my time here, and I will ensure a smooth handover before leaving.`
    

> **落款**：`Yours sincerely,`（或 `Yours,`）换行 `Li Ming`（**署名后不加句号**）

### 2. 底盘二：被动回复信

> **称呼**：`Dear [Name],`

**首段（回应来信 + 完整主题词 + 写信目的）**：

- **答复求助/提建议**：`I was delighted to receive your email about [完整主题，如 preparing for your oral report on an ancient Chinese scientist]. I am writing to share several practical suggestions with you.`
    
- **答复咨询**：`Thank you for your email inquiring about [完整主题]. I am glad to provide you with the relevant details.`
    
- **答复邀请（接受）**：`Thank you for your kind invitation to [活动全称]. I would be delighted to attend.`
    
- **答复邀请（婉拒）**：`Thank you for your kind invitation to [活动全称]. Much as I would love to attend, I regret to inform you that I cannot make it due to [具体原因].`
    
- **答复投诉**：`Thank you for bringing your concern regarding [问题事项] to our attention. Please accept our sincere apologies for the inconvenience caused.`
    

**尾段（呼应前文具体内容，避免泛用 `good news`）**：

- **通用答复**：`I hope these suggestions / details will be helpful to you. I am looking forward to [具体期待，如 reading your draft / seeing you at the event].`
    
- **婉拒补救**：`Once again, I apologize for missing this opportunity, and I wish the event every success.`
    
- **答复投诉**：`We value your feedback and hope this solution resolves the issue satisfactorily. Please let us know if there is anything else we can assist you with.`
    

> **落款**：`Yours,`（或 `Yours sincerely,`）换行 `Li Ming`

### 3. 底盘三：通知（Notice）

> **标题（居中）**：`Notice`

**首段（宣布事由 + 完整项目/活动名称 + 通知目的）**：

- **招募类**：`It is a pleasure to inform everyone that we are recruiting [a student / volunteers] for [完整项目名称]. This notice outlines the duties and requirements of the position.`
    
- **活动/讲座类**：`We are pleased to announce that [活动/讲座全称] will be held at [地点] at [时间] on [日期]. This notice is intended to provide the relevant details.`
    

**尾段（材料 + 渠道 + 截止时间三件套 + 正式收尾）**：

- **招募类**：`If you are interested in this position, please send your resume to [虚构学术邮箱，如 liming@university.edu] before [截止日期]. We look forward to your application.`
    
- **活动/讲座类**：`Those who wish to participate are welcome to sign up at [地点/邮箱] before [截止日期]. Your active participation will be highly appreciated.`
    

> **落款（右下对齐）**： `Li Ming`（或题目指定组织，如 `The Student Union`） `[Month Day, Year]`（如 `December 20, 2026`）

### 4. 底盘四：纪要（Minutes）

```
Minutes of the Meeting on [会议主题]

Date: [Month Day, Year]
Time: [起止时间，如 2:00 p.m. – 4:00 p.m.]
Venue: [地点，如 Room 302, Teaching Building]
Attendees: [参会人员]
Recorder: Li Ming
```

**正文（3—4 句客观陈述，全程过去时）**：

- **开篇背景**：`The meeting was held to discuss [会议核心目的].`
    
- **要点与决议**：`First, [主语] reviewed / proposed [议题一], and it was agreed that [决议一]. Second, the participants discussed [议题二] and decided to [具体行动].`
    
- **分工与后续**：`Finally, [责任人/小组] was assigned to [具体任务] before [截止日期].`
    

## 三、中间段“九宫格”功能句库（升级版）

中间段固定写 **4—5 句**，按题目要求的要点从下表挑选对应维度拼装。

|   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|
|**逻辑维度**|**格 1：起手核心点**|**格 2：展开 / 规则与角色**|**格 3：切换路标 / 深化收口**|
|**一、是什么**<br><br>  <br><br>_（安排、规则、职责、要求）_<br><br>  <br><br>适用：通知、邀请、咨询回复|**时间地点与核心侧重**<br><br>  <br><br>`Scheduled for [日期/时间] at [地点], this [competition / event / workshop] emphasizes [核心主题/能力，如 innovative thinking and hands-on problem-solving skills].`|**流程 / 组队规则 / 嘉宾角色**<br><br>  <br><br>• `Each team may comprise [数量] [成员]（或 consist of...）, and all entries should be submitted before [日期].`<br><br>  <br><br>• `Distinguished experts and scholars from across the globe will serve as [judges / keynote speakers / mentors].`<br><br>  <br><br>• `The selected candidate will assist in [职责1, V-ing] and [职责2, V-ing].`|**显式切换第二要点（如要求）**<br><br>  <br><br>`In terms of requirements, applicants should be enthusiastic about [领域] and possess strong [某项能力，如 analytical and problem-solving skills].`|
|**二、为什么**<br><br>  <br><br>_（双向价值、理由、原因）_<br><br>  <br><br>适用：邀请、推荐、感谢、道歉、投诉、婉拒、申请|**正面理由 / 突出优势 / 申请匹配度**<br><br>  <br><br>• `First of all, [对象] is widely admired for [特点], which can help you [好处].`<br><br>  <br><br>• `With a solid background in [领域] and experience in [经历], I am confident that I am well-suited for this role.`|**问题经过 / 客观原因**<br><br>  <br><br>`Unfortunately, [问题/冲突] occurred because [原因], causing [后果].`|**双向价值 / 实际收获（高分零错句）**<br><br>  <br><br>• `Participating（或 Attending this event）will not only enrich [对方群体]'s practical experience, but also foster meaningful [交流类型] between [双方主体].`<br><br>  <br><br>• `Thanks to your guidance, I not only [收获1], but also [收获2].`|
|**三、怎么办**<br><br>  <br><br>_（建议、补救、诉求）_<br><br>  <br><br>适用：建议、答复求助、道歉、投诉|**首要建议**<br><br>  <br><br>`First of all, it is advisable for you to [做法1], which can help you [目的1].`|**进阶建议 / 聚焦重点**<br><br>  <br><br>`Additionally, it is wise to focus mainly on [重点], as this makes [对象] more focused and engaging.`|**提供帮助 / 补救或诉求**<br><br>  <br><br>• `Finally, feel free to share [材料] with me so that I can offer more specific advice.`<br><br>  <br><br>• `To make up for this, I would like to [补救措施].`<br><br>  <br><br>• `Therefore, I request that you [动词原形，如 replace the item / refund the money].`|

### 附：“双向价值句”高频场景速查表

直接代入九宫格“为什么·格 3”的 `Participating / Joining will not only [A], but also [B].` 结构：

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|**适用场景**|**收益 A（对方/个体收获）**|**收益 B（双方/长远价值）**|
|**跨校学术/竞赛邀请**|`enrich your students' practical experience`|`foster meaningful academic collaboration between our institutions`|
|**科研助手招募通知**|`gain valuable hands-on research experience`|`enhance your analytical and problem-solving skills`|
|**文化交流/校园活动**|`broaden participants' cultural horizons`|`strengthen mutual understanding between students from diverse backgrounds`|

## 四、高复用亮点词伙库

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|**场景分类**|**推荐表达**|**中文释义与避坑提示**|
|**学术与竞赛**|`innovative thinking and hands-on problem-solving skills`|创新思维与实操解决问题的能力（替代中式 `innovation skills`）|
|**学术与竞赛**|`serve as judges / keynote speakers / mentors`|担任评委 / 主旨演讲嘉宾 / 导师（替代空泛夸赞专家的冗长定语从句）|
|**学术与竞赛**|`contribute your invaluable guidance`|贡献您极其宝贵的指导（`invaluable` = 极其宝贵的，并非“无价值”）|
|**学术与竞赛**|`foster meaningful academic collaboration`|促进富有意义的学术合作（比单方面说“为我校发展作贡献”更平等得体）|
|**科研与调研**|`conduct surveys and collect/analyze data`|开展调查并收集/分析数据（`survey` 用复数，介词后全变 `-ing`）|
|**口头报告/建议**|`focus mainly on sth.` / `rehearse your presentation in advance`|主要聚焦于…… / 提前演练展示（替代错误的 `spare most time on`）|
|**口头报告/建议**|`anecdotes about...` / `capture your audience's attention`|关于……的趣闻 / 吸引听众注意（`audience` 用单数所有格 `audience's`）|

## 五、典型真题拼装对照与成品样板

### 真题拼装速查

- **2024 答复建议信**（为留学生准备中国古代科学家口头报告提建议）：**底盘二（答复求助）+ 九宫格“怎么办”3 格**。首段必须落地 `your oral report on an ancient Chinese scientist`，防止后文代词指代悬空。
    
- **2023 招募通知**（为史密斯教授校园体育活动研究项目招募一名学生）：**底盘三（招募类）+ 九宫格“是什么”格 2（职责）与格 3（要求）**。用 `In terms of requirements` 显式切分职责与要求两个得分点。
    
- **2022 正式邀请信**（邀请英国大学教授组队参加我校国际创新大赛）：**底盘一（正式邀请信·代表视角）+ 九宫格“是什么”格 1—2（时间、侧重、人数、评委）+ “为什么”格 3（双向价值）**。
    

### 2022 正式邀请信整合成品样板（正文 108 词）

> Dear Professor,
> 
> **On behalf of** the Student Union, **it is my honor to invite you to** organize a team for the international innovation contest **to be held** at our university.
> 
> **Scheduled for December 20**, this competition **emphasizes** innovative thinking and **hands-on problem-solving skills**, and each team may **comprise** three to five students. **Distinguished experts and scholars from across the globe will serve as judges.** **Participating will not only enrich your students' practical experience but also foster meaningful academic collaboration between our institutions.**
> 
> We truly hope you will accept this invitation and **contribute your invaluable guidance**. **I would be grateful if you could let us know your decision at your earliest convenience.**
> 
> Yours sincerely, Li Ming

## 六、考场零失分避坑核对清单

写完小作文后留 **1 分钟**按以下 8 项逐条扫查：

1. **首段主题词全量落地**：把题干限定词原样或准确改写放进第 1 段，再在后文使用 `it / they / them / the scientist`，彻底杜绝指代悬空。
    
2. **动宾补结构防漏 `to`**：写到 `need / allow / enable / encourage / invite / expect` 后，立刻检查是否漏掉 `to + 动词原形`（切勿写 `need organize`、`allow you contribute`）。
    
3. **逻辑主语与悬垂修饰语**：开头用 `As a member of...,` 时，主句主语必须是人（`I am honored to...`）；若想用 `it is my honor to...`，开头改用 `On behalf of...`。
    
4. **单复数前后一致**：招募“一名学生（`a student`）”时，职责主语用单数 `The selected candidate`，泛指所有报名者用复数 `applicants`；动名词作主语（`Participating...`）接一般现在时谓语用单数。
    
5. **动词形态、语态与情态动词配对**：
    
    - 介词后并列动词同步变 `-ing`（`assist in conducting surveys, collecting and analyzing data`），`while` 省略主语后用 `-ing`（`while allowing`）。
        
    - 被动语态用过去分词（`are listed`，勿写 `can be list`）；计划将来举办的活动用 `to be held`（勿写 `would be held`）。
        
    - 委婉虚拟句配对 `I would be grateful if you could...`（勿写 `if you can`）；祝愿句补全主语用 `I hope ... will help you`（勿写 `would help`）。
        
6. **`comprise` 与 `consist of` 勿混用**：`A comprises B`（主动，**不加 `of`**）= `A consists of B`（主动，**必加 `of`**），切勿拼出 `comprise of`。
    
7. **高频易错固定搭配与拼写**：
    
    - `It is a pleasure to...`（勿漏冠词 `a`）
        
    - `recruitment`（名词：招募活动）区别于 `recruit`（动词：招募；名词：新成员）
        
    - `several`（勿误拼为 `serval`）、`invaluable`（极其宝贵的）
        
    - `focus mainly on` / `spend most of your time on`（替代错误的 `spare most time on` 与生硬的 `badly in need of doing`）
        
8. **格式与语域细节**：致信教授称呼用逗号（`Dear Professor,`），结尾慎用带催促感的 `Kindly + 动词原形`；署名 `Li Ming` 后**不加句号**；通知不用感叹号，联系邮箱写虚构学术邮箱（如 `liming@university.edu`）。
