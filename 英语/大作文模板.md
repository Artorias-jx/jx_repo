## 零、 动笔前 2 分钟：审题与梳理思路（防偏题、防卡壳）

> **核心原则**：彻底戒掉“先想复杂中文再逐字直译”。严格按 **“切分靶心 → 确定主体 → 以英定中”** 三步在草稿纸上拉出 4 行英文词块，再做语法填空。

### 第一步（30 秒）：切分图文，锁死二、三段分工
1. **动态变化图（如毕业生去向）**：只抓**变化最剧烈的单项**进第二段写原因，不平均用力。
2. **静态多分类图（如旅游目的）**：按数据大小切两栏——**头部高占比项进第二段写原因，尾部低占比项留到第三段写改进建议**（绝不把 6% 的冷门项当成主流成因）。
3. **多维交叉表（如员工满意度）**：果断舍弃“不清楚/中立”项，直接锁死**正反两头极值**，第二段写两类群体的处境对比。
4. **图画 / 混合题**：先读**气泡或标牌中文文字**定核心主题，不脱离文字自行脑补。
5. **同步定基调**（决定第三段开头用哪句）：正向进步 / 中性客观 / 负面问题。

### 第二步（45 秒）：向下钻一层，消灭车轱辘话
- **第二段选 3 个角度（不重复同一层面）**：
  1. **外部推力 / 现状**（政策扶持、社会需求、群体热衷/痛点）$\to$ 调**方案一 / 方案三**
  2. **核心价值 / 双重益处**（对个人或大众带来的具体好处）$\to$ 调**方案二 / 方案四**
  3. **对比凸显 / 反面警醒**（对比次要项优势，或反面假设上高度）$\to$ 调**方案五 / 方案六**
- **第三段执行“标题主体认领制”**：图表标题出现谁，对策必须写到谁（公司调查必写 `enterprises`；高校调查必写 `universities`；公共民生必写 `authorities + media / public`）。

### 第三步（45 秒）：以英定中 + 4 行草稿纸提纲
- **中文降维法**：遇到抽象中文（如“内卷”“上有老下有小”“竞争力”），先降维成大白话“**谁 + 动作 + 宾语**”（`face heavy pressure` / `support children and elderly parents` / `global competitiveness`），再用下方 **5 组万能连接动词 + 六大方面名词块**直接拼装。
- **草稿纸只写 4 行**：
  1. **图表类型 + 主题 3 个替身词**（如 `pie chart`；`traveling / going on a trip / sightseeing`）
  2. **首段数据 / 画面核心词**（仅记数字与名词化主语）
  3. **二段 3 个槽位英文词块**（对应选好的 3 个方案）
  4. **三段 2 个责任主体 + 动词词块**

## 一、 第一段：图文 / 图表描述句（全题型覆盖）

> **首段控字数铁律（50—55 词，最多 3 句话）**：
> 1. **先确认图名**：饼图 `pie chart`、柱状图 `bar chart`、折线图 `line chart`、表格 `table`（拿不准统一写 **`chart`**，绝不张冠李戴）。
> 2. **数据项直接名词化**：用 `employment`、`further study`、`going sightseeing` 直接作主语，**不要写**冗长的 `the percentage of students who choose to...`。
> 3. **防首尾重复**：若第 1 句已点明核心趋势，报完数据直接换行进第二段，省略通用收尾句。

### 1. 纯图表类（5 套模板）

- **模板一：动态 · 单向趋势型（单条线持续上升 / 下降）**
  - **① 破题句**：*As illustrated in the chart, the period from [起始年份] to [结束年份] witnessed a steady rise / decline in [主题词].*
  - **② 细节句（注意过去时态）**：*The rate increased gradually from [数据1] in [年份1] to [数据2] in [年份2]. Over the following [数字] years, it surged notably to [数据3].*

- **模板二：动态 · 多向对比型（有升有降，对比鲜明）**
  - **① 破题句**：*As illustrated in the chart, the period from [起始年份] to [结束年份] saw divergent trends in [主题词], with [上升项] rising while [下降项] falling.*
  - **② 细节句（名词化 + `while` 合并，严控 2 句内）**：*Specifically, [名词化项目A] decreased remarkably from [数据1] to [数据2], while [名词化项目B] climbed significantly from [数据3] to [数据4]. Meanwhile, [名词化项目C] rose slightly from [数据5] to [数据6].*

- **模板三：动态 · 单点突出型（突出变化最剧烈的单项）**
  - **① 破题句**：*As illustrated in the chart, the period from [起始年份] to [结束年份] saw notable changes in [主题词], among which the rise in [单项名词短语] was the most striking.*
  - **② 细节句**：多项对比套用**模板二 ②**；单线多年份套用**模板一 ②**。

- **模板四：静态 · 单一占比 / 表格对比型（无年份变化，如饼图 / 表格）**
  - **① 破题句**：*As illustrated in the pie chart / table, there are noticeable differences in [主题词], with [最高项] ranking first (or: [核心对比]).*
  - **② 细节句（⚠️ 复数主语配 `account`，不加 `s`）**：
    - *饼图/柱图*：*To be specific, [项目A] and [项目B] **account** for [数据A] and [数据B] respectively, while [项目C] **accounts** for [数据C].*
    - *满意度/支持率表格*：*To be specific, [群体A] report the highest satisfaction rate at [数据A], while [群体B] express the strongest dissatisfaction at [数据B].*

- **模板五：双图组合型（左右 / 上下图对比）**
  - **① 破题句**：*The pie chart on the left shows [左图主题], while the bar chart on the right illustrates [右图主题].*
  - **② 细节句**：沿用**模板四 ②**报左图核心数据，右图最高项放进下方收尾句。

- **纯图表通用收尾句（仅在破题句未点明核心结论，或双图需引出右图数据时使用）**：
  - *It is apparent from the statistics that [核心主体] has / have drawn growing attention from the public, with [核心因素/特征] ranking as the top priority (at [核心数据]).*
  - *注：中性考量用 `top priority`；负面隐患用 `top concern`。*

### 2. 图画与混合类（英语一高频题型）

- **模板六：纯图画型（漫画 + 气泡 / 标题文字）**
  - **① 画面句**：*As is symbolically depicted in the cartoon above, [核心人物 + 正在进行的动作], with [伴随场景/人物 doing].*
  - **② 寓意句**：*From the dialogue / caption in the picture, we can see that [主题词] is drawing growing attention from the public, which deserves our continued support.*

- **模板七：图画 + 图表混合型**
  - **① 混合描述句（必译文字 + 必报起止数字）**：*The cartoon on the left depicts a lively scene where [画面人物动作及标牌关键词], while the chart on the right illustrates a steady rise / decline in [图表主题] from [数据1] in [年份1] to [数据2] in [年份2].*
  - **② 寓意句**：*Without a doubt, the drawing and chart emphasize the fact that [核心成就 / 社会现象].*

## 二、 第二段：原因与现象剖析句（六大实战方案，任选 3 个拼装）

> **固定段落总起句（根据题型切换末尾词）**：
> *A combination of factors **has** contributed to the trend / phenomenon reflected in the **chart(s) / cartoon / picture and chart**.*
>
> **固定衔接词组（锁定零错）**：**`First and foremost, ... Furthermore, ... Last but not least, ...`**

### 方案一：现状 / 举例切入（适合第一条原因：政策推动 / 群体现状）
- **核心骨架**：*For instance / To give a common example, **[具体主体，如 local authorities / many communities / young people]** **[态度/行动短语]** **[主题/投入词块]**, which enables **[受益群体]** to **[具体动作/好处]**. (可选加长：Consequently, **[结果，如 public satisfaction / efficiency]** has improved noticeably.)*
- **高频态度/行动短语槽位**：
  - 高度重视（政府/机构）：`place a high priority on` / `attach great importance to`
  - 兴趣与投入（个人/群体）：`be passionate about`（热衷于）/ `be occupied with`（忙于）/ `dedicate oneself to doing`（致力于）
  - 负面痛点（压力/厌倦）：`be bored with`（厌倦）/ `be indifferent to`（漠不关心）/ `face heavy pressure from`（面临重压）
- **社会需求变体（适合升学/求职/科技类）**：
  - *First and foremost, rapid social development demands more **[high-level talent / technological innovation]** to enhance **[our country's global competitiveness]**, which motivates **[graduates]** to **[pursue further education]**.*

### 方案二：群众观点 + 非限制性定语从句（最万能，按话题二选一）
- **观点引出动词替换**：`believe` $\to$ **`hold the view` / `maintain` / `are convinced`**
- **分支 A：成长与竞争类（⚠️ 仅限学习、升学、求职、合作、技能提升）**
  - *The majority of **[students / individuals]** **hold the view** that **[主题词]** will **create more opportunities for them** (or: provide them with a broader platform) to **[develop themselves / acquire practical skills]**, which **is likely to secure a competitive edge** (or: put them in a **favorable** position) in the future.*
- **分支 B：生活与福祉类（⚠️ 百搭文化、旅游、健康、公园、养老、环保）**
  - *The majority of people **are convinced** that **[主题词/活动]** can **[好处1，如 relieve stress]** and **[好处2，如 enrich their spiritual life]**, which helps enhance **[目标词块，如 their physical and mental well-being / a sense of fulfillment]**.*

### 方案三：万能从句壳 + 核心关系短语（正反话题通吃，防翻译卡壳）
- **三选一从句壳（拉长句子、零报错）**：
  1. *It is generally agreed that...*（人们普遍认同……）
  2. *There is no doubt that...*（毫无疑问……）
  3. *A rising number of individuals are coming to the realization that...*（越来越多的人开始意识到……）
- **四选一核心关系短语（⚠️ 注意主语单复数配动词三单）**：
  - **关键作用**：`[主题词] **plays a crucial role in** [某方面，如 fostering cultural confidence / supporting families]`
  - **积极贡献**：`[主题词] **contributes significantly to** [某方面，如 social harmony / personal growth]`
  - **消极危害（负面题王牌）**：`[负面现象] **is seriously destructive to** [某方面，如 interpersonal ties / mental health]`（平替：`poses a tremendous threat to`）
  - **高度依赖**：`[重要方面，如 high-quality development] **is strongly dependent on** [主题词]`
- **观念转变变体**：*People's mindset has shifted noticeably from **[旧观念，如 merely curing illnesses]** to **[新观念，如 actively preventing them]**, which encourages them to **[具体积极行为]**.*

### 方案四：`Not only... but also...` 部分倒装（双益处加分句，适合第二条原因）
- **单数主语 / 动名词公式**：*Not only **does** **[主题词/动名词]** **[动词原形1：enhance / promote / broaden / boost / relieve]** **[方面1]**, but **it** also **[三单动词2：enables sb. to do / enriches / cultivates]** **[方面2]**.*
- **复数主语公式（⚠️ 防主谓不一致）**：*Not only **do** **[复数主题，如 outdoor activities]** **[动词原形1]** **[方面1]**, but **they** also **[动词原形2]** **[方面2]**.*
- **例句**：*Not only does traveling broaden students' horizons, but it also relieves daily fatigue and **enriches** their lives.*

### 方案五：对比论证（突出高占比项 / 新方式的优势，替换 `so as to`）
- **公式 1（`Compared with` + `thereby doing`，已修正 `so as to` 语病）**：
  - *Compared with **[次要项/旧方式 B，如 employment / traditional human care]**, **[主要项/新事物 A，如 pursuing a higher degree]** can **[小好处，如 expand their horizons / offer 24-hour assistance]**, **thereby [大好处 doing，如 promoting all-round development / enhancing quality of life]**.*
- **公式 2（`In contrast to` 极简高分句）**：
  - *In contrast to **[次要项 B]**, the advantage of **[主要项 A]** lies in **[名词短语/动名词，如 getting close to nature / offering higher efficiency]**.*

### 方案六：反面假设 / 警醒收尾（建议放在第三条原因，按层次三选一）
> **⚠️ 时态铁律**：下面默认给出**零失误真实条件版（`原形 + will`）**；若想写**虚拟语气版**，将从句动词改为过去式（`ignored / failed / underestimated`）、主句 `will` 改为 `would`，**严禁混搭！**
> **⚠️ 分寸提醒**：小话题（如旅游、公园）用括号内的温和具体后果，不要强行“扣帽子”说国家落后。

- **个人层**：*If we ignore the importance of **[主题词]**, we **will** never become a better version of ourselves (or: **will** lack **[a sense of well-being / work-life balance]**).*
- **社会层**：*If everyone fails to **[具体动作]**, our society **will** fail to achieve **[sustainable development / social harmony]** (重磅话题可用：be confronted with an unpromising and fruitless future).*
- **国家层**：*If we underestimate the importance of **[主题词]**, our nation **will** lose its **[cultural roots / global competitiveness]** (重磅科技/人才话题可用：permanently lag behind in the new era of rapid development).*

## 三、 第三段：总结定性与对策句（骨架 + 槽位）

### 1. 段落总结句（按题目属性五选一）
- **正向进步类（主力默认，辩证递进）**：*From what has been analyzed above, it is clear that while significant progress has been made, continuous efforts are still required.*
- **中性趋势预测类（升学、择业、旅游等）**：*To sum up, it is not difficult to draw the conclusion that **[动名词/现象，如 pursuing further education]** will become increasingly common among **[群体]**.*（⚠️ 绝不写 `the proportion ... will become common`）
- **负面问题类专用**：*From what has been analyzed above, it is clear that this issue has become increasingly prominent, and continuous efforts are still required.*
- **强调必要性**：*To sum up, the discussion leads us to realize that **[核心事物复数]** are not a luxury / an option, but a necessity.*
- **否定倒装强调重要性（加分句）**：*To sum up, **under no circumstances can we underestimate** the profound impact of **[主题词]**.*

### 2. 多主体对策句（按“标题主体认领制”选 2 句）
- **政府 / 监管部门**：*For one thing, it is imperative for the authorities to enact strict regulations to ensure **[information security / privacy protection / public health security / employees' rights and interests]**.*
- **企业 / 高校**：*For one thing, enterprises / universities ought to spare no effort to **[improve workplace welfare / organize study tours / improve product quality]**.*
- **媒体 / 平台**：*For another, new media platforms should appeal to the public to **[explore traditional customs / raise environmental awareness / embrace a healthy lifestyle]**.*
- **个人 / 学生 / 员工**：*For another, **[students / employees / individuals]** should **[choose wisely based on their career goals / adjust their mindset and pursue a work-life balance / cultivate independence]**.*

### 3. 结尾升华倒装句（二选一）
- **宏观社会版**：*Only through the joint efforts of all parties (or: our joint efforts) can we effectively promote **[宏观目标词块，如 sustainable development / cultural prosperity / social harmony / high-quality development]** in the long run.*
- **个人成长极简版**：*Only in this way can we / they **embrace** a brighter future.*

## 五、 考场 1 分钟专项排雷清单（锁死零硬伤）

1. **主谓一致 3 条铁律（最高频失血点）**：
   - `A combination of factors` 永远配单数 **`has`**。
   - `A and B` 并列动名词作主语配复数（**`account`**）；单个动名词短语作主语配单数（**`enables`**）。
   - `Not only does [单数] ..., but it also [三单动词]`；复数换 `do ... they`。
2. **时态双重锁死**：
   - `if` 条件句严禁混搭：只用 `ignore ... will` 或 `ignored ... would`。
   - 过去年份图表全程用一般过去时（或直接名词化砍掉定语从句）。
3. **固定搭配、冠词与标点**：
   - 锁定搭配：**`for the sake of`**（不是 `seek`）、**`most college students`**（不写 `the most of`）、**`satisfied with`**（不是 `about`）、**`common among`**（不是 `of`）、**`elderly parents`**（不是 `elder`）、**`rights and interests`**（复数）、**`appeal to sb. to do`**（不漏 `to`）、**`spare no effort`**（`effort` 不加 `s`）。
   - 冠词与标点：抽象不可数名词泛指不加 `the`（`traditional culture` / `beautiful scenery`）；图中特指单数必加 `the`（`the dialogue`）；句首 `However,` 后必加逗号。
   - 逻辑抽查：`proportion` 配 `rise`，不配 `common`；`talent` 作“人才总称”不可数（`top talent`）。
4. **零缩略词与易错词形拼写**：
   - 杜绝一切缩略词：`What's` $\to$ `What is`、`It's` $\to$ `It is`、`won't` $\to$ `will not`。
   - 词尾变形自查：`-ch` 动词三单加 `-es`（**`enriches`**）；辅音 `+ y` 变 `-ies`（**`universities`**、**`facilities`**）。
   - 形近/易颠倒词黑名单：**`satisfaction`**（别多写 `i`）、**`mindset`**（别写 `mindest`）、**`broaden`**（别写 `boarden`）、**`embrace`**（别写 `brace`）、**`pursue`**（别写 `purse`）、**`platform`**（别写 `platfrom`）、**`continuous`**（别漏 `u`）。
