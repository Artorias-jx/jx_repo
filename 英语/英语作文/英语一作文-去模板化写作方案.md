# 英语一作文 · 去模板化写作方案

> **这份文件解决一个问题**：怎么用一套准备好的东西，写出**不像模板**的文章，并且能反复用、适配不同题。
> **内容来源**：反模板方法论为我的总结；「预审题」概念、句位变体中的部分表达取自你提供的材料（御风「预审题」理论）。星级以外的判断未经过阅卷数据验证。

---

## 使用说明 · 三句话说清

1. **这份文件不提供"整篇模板"，只提供"碎片"**——骨架 4 套、功能位 9 组、每组 4–5 个变体。
2. **组合自由度就是反模板能力**：9 个功能位各选一个，理论组合数上万，且每个变体风格不同。
3. **固定 2–3 个"个人标记"**，让它出现在你每篇文章里——这会把"模板痕迹"变成"个人风格"。

**与另一份文件的分工**（`考研英语一作文-精选整合模板.md`）：
那份是"背什么"（骨架句、语料、范文）；这份是"怎么变"（骨架可换、句位可换、轮换规则）。两份配合使用。

# 第一章 诊断：模板为什么会被看出来

## 1.1 阅卷场景

一位阅卷老师 30 秒读完一篇作文，一上午读几百篇。**他识别模板不靠分析，靠"既视感"**——某个句式他今天已经见过 80 次了。

这决定了反模板的核心：**不是把句子写得更好，而是写得跟别人不一样。**

## 1.2 六个露馅信号（附反例）

| # | 信号 | 典型表现 | 为什么露馅 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **首句雷同** | `Amid the vivid imagery displayed, a snapshot of a prevailing phenomenon emerges.` / `As is vividly depicted in the picture...` | 这几个开头已被全网背烂，老师今天见过上百次 |
| 2 | **过渡词机械** | `To begin with... Additionally... More significantly...` 三段连用 | 这种"教科书式三段递进"只出现在作文里，真人写作不会这么齐整 |
| 3 | **万能原因兜底** | `With the rapid development of society and economy...` | 与题目零关联，放哪道题都成立 = 放哪道题都不成立 |
| 4 | **为句式而句式** | 强行倒装、强行强调句，与内容脱节 | 语法结构正确但逻辑突兀，是"表演"不是"表达" |
| 5 | **术语空转** | 通篇 cultural heritage / sustainable development，却不解释它与画面哪一点相关 | 内容优质但与题目无关 |
| 6 | **段落等长** | 三段各 3 句、各 70 词 | 真实写作的段落长短是跟着内容走的 |

> **一句话总结**：模板感 = 内容与题目脱钩 + 句式与内容脱钩 + 与全体考生雷同。

## 1.3 反模板三原则

**原则一：内容先行，句式后置**
先问自己"这道题到底在说什么、我要说什么观点"，观点定了再选句式。
- ❌ 错误顺序：先想"我要用哪个倒装句" → 再往里塞题目内容
- ✅ 正确顺序：先陈述观点（哪怕用最简单的句子）→ 再选一个合适的句式承载它

**原则二：具体优于抽象**
抽象概括是模板的温床。
- 模板：*This reflects the great progress of our society.*
- 自然：*Two decades ago few village families could afford a trip to the county town; now they send their children to universities in other provinces.*

**原则三：不齐整优于齐整**
真人写作有长短句交错、有不完全对称的过渡。**偶尔用一个平实的短句（`That is a real change.`）反而更像人写的。**

# 第二章 骨架层：可换的文章结构

固定"描述—分析—结论"是模板感的来源之一。下面每类题型给 **3–4 套骨架**，按题目特点选。

## 2.1 图画作文 · 四套骨架

### 骨架 A｜画面 → 主旨 → 双因 → 升华（常规稳妥）
- 段 1：描述画面 + 一句话点出主旨
- 段 2：两个角度分析（每角度 2 句）
- 段 3：总结 + 建议/呼吁

**适用**：主题抽象、有话可说的题（坚持、心态、选择、文化）。
**优点**：稳，不会写崩。
**缺点**：最常用，需要靠句位变体拉开差距。

### 骨架 B｜细节 → 追问 → 作答 → 回扣（适合信息量小的纯图画）
- 段 1：抓住画面**某一个细节**，提出一个**具体的问题**（不是泛泛的"它说明了什么"）
- 段 2：正面回答这个问题（原因 + 影响）
- 段 3：回扣画面细节，给出可操作的行动

**适用**：2023 龙舟、2021 自我与他人这类画面简单、留白大的题。
**优点**：显得在"想问题"而不是在"套框架"。
**风险**：问题要问得具体，问不好会显得空。

> 例（2023 龙舟）段 1 收尾可用：*What accounts for the survival of a custom that many expected to fade?*

### 骨架 C｜对照 → 分歧根源 → 倾向判断（适合双图对比 / 观点对立）
- 段 1：两方对照 + 点出**分歧点**
- 段 2：解释分歧为什么存在（两方各自合理之处）
- 段 3：给出你的倾向性判断 + 理由

**适用**：2020 两种习惯、2022 听讲座、2014 母女相携。
**优点**：天然有思辨感，阅卷老师喜欢"两方都看到了"的写法。

### 骨架 D｜让步 → 转折 → 深化（适合容易写空的题目）
- 段 1：描述 + 承认一个表面现象
- 段 2：先让步（`It is true that...` 承认普遍说法）+ 再转折（更深层其实是……）
- 段 3：结论

**适用**：题目看似简单、容易写成口号时（如 2023 龙舟、2021 热爱）。
**优点**：立刻显出思想层次，最不像模板。
**风险**：转折要真的转得动，转不动就别用。

## 2.2 图表作文 · 三套骨架

### 骨架 A｜趋势主导（动态图：折线、多年柱状）
- 段 1：一句总趋势 + 一句极值（**数据集中在这一段**）
- 段 2：原因（2 个）+ 影响（1 个）
- 段 3：判断 + 建议（**不出现数字**）

**适用**：2023 英二健康素养、2025 英一家电拥有量。

### 骨架 B｜矛盾主导（静态图：单年柱状、饼图、表格）
- 段 1：点出**最突出的一项** + 与之形成反差的项（用 while 制造对比）
- 段 2：解释这种分布**为什么**形成 + 这种分布**意味着什么**
- 段 3：判断 + 建议

**适用**：2025 英二社区老人休闲、2016 英二旅游目的。
**关键**：静态图命题人没给角度，**"分类 + 制造对比冲突"是你自己找出来的角度**——这正是拿分点。

### 骨架 C｜多维并置（双图 / 多指标）
- 段 1：两图各一句 + 一句**关联句**（两图在说什么同一件事）
- 段 2：分别解读 + 指出两图之间的张力（如"接受度高但顾虑也高"）
- 段 3：综合判断

**适用**：2026 英一养老机器人（饼图+柱状）。
**关键**：衔接两图的那一句是全场最重要的句子，要写好。

## 2.3 文字材料作文 · 两套骨架

### 骨架 A｜改写 → 表态 → 论证 → 结论（通用）
段 1：改写材料 + 明确表态；段 2：论证；段 3：结论。

### 骨架 B｜质疑 → 辨析 → 立论（适合材料观点有明显立场时）
段 1：改写 + 指出这句话的**前提**；段 2：辨析这个前提在什么情况下成立、什么情况下不成立；段 3：给出你的条件化结论。

> 骨架 B 更高级，但需要材料本身有可辨析的空间。

## 2.4 骨架使用规则

| 规则 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| **连续两篇不重复** | 上一篇用了骨架 A，下一篇换 B 或 C |
| **按题目特征选** | 信息量小 → B；双图对比 → C；容易写空 → D |
| **一篇内不混用** | 选了骨架就贯彻到底，混用会散 |
| **段数可调** | 三段是默认，但段 2 强时可写两段分析（共四段），段 2 弱时并入段 3（共两段） |

# 第三章 句位层：功能句变体库 ★核心

> **这一章是整份方案的核心。** 每个功能位给 4–5 个变体，风格刻意做得不同。
> 标注说明：`【风格】` 决定它适合什么文章；`【风险】` 提示别什么时候用。

## 3.1 图画描述导入（以"2023 龙舟"为例示范）

**M1-A｜直陈式（最自然，首选）**
> *A crowd has gathered along a village river to watch a dragon boat race, while two elderly residents nearby remark with delight that the event draws more participants every year.*

【风格】平实、直接进入画面。**这是最不像模板的写法**，因为没有"引入套话"。
【适用】任何图画题，尤其信息量充足时。
【风险】无。**强烈建议把这一式作为主力**。

**M1-B｜氛围式（以地点或声音起笔）**
> *Cheered on from both banks, rowers drive their boats through the water in a village dragon boat race — a scene the two onlookers by the river call livelier than ever.*

【风格】有画面感，略带文学性。
【适用】场景感强的图画（比赛、聚会、街头）。
【风险】氛围描写别超过一句，否则挤占议论篇幅。

**M1-C｜反差式（从"过去 vs 现在"切入）**
> *Once a fading custom kept alive by a few elders, the village dragon boat race now fills the river with rowers and spectators.*

【风格】有历史纵深感，天然带论点。
【适用】文化传承、社会变迁类主题。
【风险】"过去如何"是你自己补的，不能与题目冲突。

**M1-D｜引语式（从画面中人物的原话起笔）**
> *"The race here gets more crowded every year," remarks one of two elderly villagers, as boats cut through the water and spectators cheer from the banks.*

【风格】生动，像现场报道。
【适用】画面中明确有人物说话气泡（2020 习惯、2022 讲座）。
【风险】引语必须是画面里真实有的，或可合理推断的。

**M1-E｜判断先行式（先给一个定性，再描述）**
> *What the picture records is not a staged festival but a living tradition: villagers of all ages turn out for the dragon boat race, and the elderly spectators note that its popularity keeps rising.*

【风格】有观点，起笔就有立场。
【适用】你对该现象有明确判断时。
【风险】判断错了会跑偏，要先想清楚。

> ⚠️ **建议淘汰的写法**：`Amid the vivid imagery displayed...` / `As is vividly depicted in the picture...` / `The cartoon presents an encouraging scene.`
> 这三个已经是"模板身份证"，**弃用比改良更划算**。

## 3.2 图表数据导入（以"2025 英一 家电拥有量"示范）

**M2-A｜直陈指标式（首选）**
> *The table tracks the number of air conditioners, refrigerators and washing machines owned by the average Chinese household per hundred households from 2014 to 2023.*

【风格】平实准确。【风险】无，主力用法。

**M2-B｜主题先行式**
> *Rising ownership of three everyday appliances between 2014 and 2023 is the story this table tells.*

【风格】起笔即结论，利落。【适用】趋势非常明显时。【风险】总趋势判断错就全错。

**M2-C｜提问式**
> *How much has ordinary Chinese life changed in a decade? The table offers one measure: the number of air conditioners, refrigerators and washing machines per hundred households.*

【风格】有代入感。【适用】想让第一段不单调。【风险】提问要问得具体，否则显空。

**M2-D｜背景引入式**
> *As living standards have risen over the past decade, so has the ownership of everyday appliances — a shift the table quantifies for three of them between 2014 and 2023.*

【风格】自然，先给背景再给数据。【风险】背景不能喧宾夺主。

**M2-E｜双图衔接式（专用于双图题）**
> *Two charts record, respectively, how far consumers accept elderly-care robots and what worries them most — acceptance being the higher of the two.*

【风格】一句串起两图，信息密度高。【适用】2026 英一这类双图题。

## 3.3 主旨点明（第一段收尾）

**M3-A｜直陈观点**
> *The scene points to a simple truth: traditions survive when ordinary people keep them alive.*

**M3-B｜限定式（把话题收窄到可论证范围）**
> *What matters here is less the race itself than the willingness of a community to keep it going.*

**M3-C｜追问式**
> *The interesting question is not whether the custom is popular, but why it has outlived so many others.*

**M3-D｜让步转折式**
> *Impressive as the turnout is, the deeper significance lies in what it says about local identity.*

**M3-E｜双面式（为后面的"两方都看到了"铺垫）**
> *Behind the cheerful scene lies a tension worth examining: celebration on the surface, and the question of sustainability underneath.*

> ⚠️ **淘汰**：`aptly distills the essence of this issue`、`serves as a broader reflection of`——这两个短语在模板圈里出现频率太高。

## 3.4 转向议论（第一段末 → 第二段）

**M4-A｜直接起论**
> *Two factors explain this trend.*

**M4-B｜自问自答**
> *What accounts for this? The answer lies mainly in two developments.*

**M4-C｜铺垫式**
> *To understand why this matters, it helps to look at how the change has come about.*

**M4-D｜判断式**
> *This development deserves more than applause; it needs explanation.*

**M4-E｜并入式（不单独写过渡句，直接开始第一分点）**
> *The rise in appliance ownership is first of all a matter of income.*

【说明】M4-E 最自然——**很多模板文的问题就是过渡句太多**，一句接一句的"元话语"，正文反而单薄。

## 3.5 论点展开 · 原因（第二段主体）

**M5-A｜归因式**
> *The primary reason is economic: household incomes have risen faster than prices, leaving families with room to spend on comfort.*

**M5-B｜机制式（说清"怎么发生的"）**
> *Cheaper manufacturing and wider distribution networks have brought appliances within reach of families that once considered them luxuries.*

**M5-C｜对比式（用前后对比代替抽象归因）**
> *Where a single air conditioner once served an entire building, a three-bedroom flat now commonly has three.*

**M5-D｜层级式（由表及里）**
> *Superficially this is about purchasing power; more fundamentally, it reflects a shift in what people consider basic.*

**M5-E｜反事实式（用"如果没有"论证）**
> *Had appliance prices not fallen as sharply as they did, ownership figures would look very different today.*

> **关键**：这五式都必须**装具体内容**。原因句不允许出现"社会经济发展"这种万能搭配——它是模板的信号弹。

## 3.6 论点展开 · 影响与意义

**M6-A｜个体层面**
> *For ordinary families, the gain is measured in hours saved on housework each week.*

**M6-B｜社会层面**
> *At the social level, the spread of such goods reshapes how households spend their leisure.*

**M6-C｜限定式（避免夸大）**
> *The effect is real, though it should not be overstated: better appliances improve comfort rather than transform lives.*

**M6-D｜双向式（利与弊都给）**
> *Convenience, however, has a cost — energy demand rises with every unit sold.*

**M6-E｜指向未来式**
> *If the trend continues, the pressure will shift from owning appliances to using them efficiently.*

## 3.7 让步与深化（提升思辨感，非必选）

**M7-A｜让步**
> *It is true that the picture celebrates a positive development.*

**M7-B｜转折加深**
> *Yet enthusiasm alone will not keep the tradition alive; that requires structures — funding, teaching, and time.*

**M7-C｜限定条件**
> *The trend is encouraging, but only if it is accompanied by investment in maintenance and training.*

**M7-D｜回收前文**
> *This returns us to the question raised earlier: what, exactly, is being preserved?*

【用法】放在第二段末尾或第三段开头。**一篇用一次就够**，用两次会显得刻意。

## 3.8 结论

**M8-A｜总括式**
> *Taken together, the figures describe a decade of steady improvement in ordinary life.*

**M8-B｜判断式**
> *The change is substantial, and it is not merely a matter of statistics.*

**M8-C｜保留式（不给绝对结论）**
> *Whether this pace can be sustained remains an open question.*

**M8-D｜回扣式（回到第一段的细节/问题）**
> *This brings us back to the two villagers by the river: what they are watching is not a festival, but a habit being passed on.*

**M8-E｜不总结、直接给建议的写法**
> *The next step is less about encouragement than about provision — of space, funds and time.*

> 图表题结论段：段首用 `Overall` 或 `On balance`，**不要用 `In conclusion`**（太模板），段内**不出现数字**。

## 3.9 建议与呼吁

**M9-A｜主体分工式**
> *Local governments can secure the funding; schools and families can supply the continuity.*

**M9-B｜条件式**
> *Progress of this kind depends less on policy documents than on whether ordinary people find it worth their time.*

**M9-C｜收束式（避免口号）**
> *None of this will happen on its own, but none of it is beyond reach either.*

**M9-D｜个人代入式（谨慎使用）**
> *As students, the most useful thing we can do is simply to take part.*

【风险】**M9-D 慎用**。`As college students, we should...` 是模板标配，一篇用一次可以，但别每篇都出现。

# 第四章 词汇层：去模板化替换

## 4.1 高频模板表达黑名单 → 替换表

| 别用（模板身份证） | 换成 |
|---|---|
| `With the rapid development of society and economy` | 直接写具体变化：*As urban incomes rose...* |
| `play an increasingly important role in` | *matters more than it used to* / *now shapes...* |
| `arouse wide concern` | *has drawn attention from...* / 直接陈述 |
| `every coin has two sides` | *the gains come with costs* |
| `as far as I am concerned` | *in my view* / *I would argue* / 直接给判断 |
| `It is universally acknowledged that` | 直接陈述，不加"众所周知"的帽子 |
| `Last but not least` | *Equally important* / 或直接开始第三点 |
| `There is no denying that` | 直接陈述 |
| `As college students, we should` | *The most useful step, for those of us still studying, is...* |
| `Only in this way can we...` | 每篇最多用一次；换 *That is the only way...* / *Nothing short of this will...* |
| `aptly distills the essence of this issue` | 直接写你的观点 |
| `Amid the vivid imagery displayed` | 见 M1-A 直陈式 |

## 4.2 动词：从"语气词"转向"描述词"

模板文爱用小词 + 副词（`is very important`），自然写作爱用**有描述性的动词**。

| 平淡 | 有描述性 |
|---|---|
| is important | matters, counts, weighs heavily |
| make... better | improve, strengthen, refine |
| make... worse | undermine, erode, weaken |
| cause | give rise to, bring about, trigger, drive |
| show | track, record, reveal, indicate, set out |
| use | draw on, rely on, turn to |
| need | calls for, requires, depends on |

## 4.3 避免"大词空转"

**大词**（cultural heritage, sustainable development, social harmony）不是不能用，而是**用之前必须交代它和题目的哪一点相关**。

❌ *The dragon boat race reflects the importance of cultural heritage.*（大词空转）
✅ *The race survives because it is embedded in village life — the same boats, the same river, the same families rowing them.*（具体化，"文化传承"的意思出来了，但不用这个词）

# 第五章 组装层：轮换矩阵

## 5.1 模块编号

| 模块 | 功能 | 变体数 | 编码 | 对应章节 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | 图画导入 | 5 | A–E | 3.1 |
| M2 | 图表导入 | 5 | A–E | 3.2 |
| M3 | 主旨点明 | 5 | A–E | 3.3 |
| M4 | 转向议论 | 5 | A–E | 3.4 |
| M5 | 原因展开 | 5 | A–E | 3.5 |
| M6 | 影响/意义 | 5 | A–E | 3.6 |
| M7 | 让步深化（可选） | 4 | A–D | 3.7 |
| M8 | 结论 | 5 | A–E | 3.8 |
| M9 | 建议呼吁 | 4 | A–D | 3.9 |

> M1 / M2 二选一（看题型）；M7 可选。
> **理论组合数**：导入(5) × 主旨(5) × 转向(5) × 原因(5) × 影响(5) × 结论(5) × 建议(4) ≈ **6.2 万**；启用 M7 则约 **25 万**。
> **实际可用**：练习时先固定 2 套，考前扩到 4 套即可。

## 5.2 轮换表（避免连续重复）

| 练习序号 | M1/M2 导入 | M3 主旨 | M4 转向 | M5 原因 | M6 影响 | M8 结论 | M9 建议 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 第 1 篇 | A | A | E | A | A | A | A |
| 第 2 篇 | C | B | A | D | C | D | B |
| 第 3 篇 | E | D | C | B | E | B | C |
| 第 4 篇 | B | E | D | E | B | C | A |
| 第 5 篇 | D | C | B | C | D | E | D |

**规则**：同一模块相邻两篇**不选同一个字母**。按这个表轮，5 篇内没有任何两篇结构雷同。

## 5.3 个人标记（关键技巧）

**做法**：挑 **2–3 个**自己不常见但用着顺手的表达，让它自然出现在每篇文章里。

**推荐类型**：
- **一个偏好连接词**：`That said,` / `Even so,` / `By the same logic,`
- **一个偏好动词**：`underscore` / `hinge on` / `stem from`
- **一个偏好句式**：`What matters more is...` / `The question is not A but B.`

**注意**：
1. **不要每篇放在同一位置**（否则又成模板）——这次放段 2 开头，下次放段 3 中间。
2. **每篇只出现 1–2 次**，多了显刻意。
3. 选完后**测试 3 篇**，确认放进不同话题都不违和。

**原理**：阅卷老师识别的是"跟别人一样"；个人标记让他识别到的是"跟别人不一样"——这时模板痕迹就被覆盖了。

# 第六章 实战演练：同一题目的多种写法

## 6.1 题目：2023 英语一（龙舟赛越来越热闹）

**Directions**：Write an essay of 160–200 words. In your essay, you should 1) describe the picture briefly, 2) interpret the implied meaning, and 3) give your comments.

### 写法一｜骨架 A + M1-A + M3-A（平实稳妥型）

> *A crowd has gathered along a village river to watch a dragon boat race, and two elderly residents nearby remark with delight that the event draws more participants every year. The scene points to a simple truth: traditions survive when ordinary people keep them alive.*
>
> *Two factors explain this revival. The first is local pride. Rowing for one's village carries a meaning that a televised race cannot replace, and younger residents increasingly want a part in it. The second is deliberate effort: schools and community groups have begun teaching drumming and boat-building, so the skills are no longer confined to a few old hands. The result is not merely a livelier festival but a network of relationships rebuilt around a shared activity.*
>
> *This revival deserves support rather than applause alone. Local governments can secure the funding; schools and families can supply the continuity. None of this will happen on its own, but none of it is beyond reach either.*

### 写法二｜骨架 D + M1-C + M3-D（让步转折型，思辨感强）

> *Once a fading custom kept alive by a few elders, the village dragon boat race now fills the river with rowers and spectators. Impressive as the turnout is, the deeper significance lies in what it says about local identity.*
>
> *It is true that the picture celebrates a positive development. A tradition that draws crowds is plainly healthier than one that survives only in textbooks. Yet enthusiasm alone will not keep it alive; that requires structures — funding, teaching, and time. Without a steady supply of young rowers and the craftsmen who build the boats, next year's festival could look very different from this one. The enthusiasm on display is therefore a starting point rather than a guarantee.*
>
> *What the picture ultimately suggests is that preserving a tradition is less a matter of sentiment than of organisation. The next step is less about encouragement than about provision — of space, funds and time.*

### 写法三｜骨架 B + M1-D + M3-C（细节切入型，最不像模板）

> *"The race here gets more crowded every year," remarks one of two elderly villagers, as boats cut through the water and spectators cheer from the banks. The interesting question is not whether the custom is popular, but why it has outlived so many others.*
>
> *Part of the answer is that the race is tied to place. It is not a performance staged for visitors but a contest between neighbouring villages, which means everyone on the bank has a stake in the outcome. Part of it is generational: grandparents rowed these boats, and their grandchildren now want to do the same. A custom embedded in this way does not need to be protected from the outside; it renews itself.*
>
> *This brings us back to the two villagers by the river: what they are watching is not a festival, but a habit being passed on.*

**三篇对比**：

| | 写法一 | 写法二 | 写法三 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 骨架 | A（常规） | D（让步转折） | B（细节切入） |
| 首句形态 | 直陈画面 | 过去 vs 现在 | 人物原话 |
| 观点强度 | 中 | 强 | 中偏强 |
| 相似度 | 中 | 低 | 最低 |
| 风险 | 最低 | 转折要转得动 | 问题要问得具体 |

## 6.2 题目：2025 英语一（家电拥有量表格）· 骨架对照

**骨架 A（趋势主导）**
段 1：*The table tracks the average number of air conditioners, refrigerators and washing machines per hundred Chinese households between 2014 and 2023. All three rose, air conditioners most sharply — from 75.2 to 145.9 units.*
段 2：原因（收入增长 + 制造与物流成本下降）→ 影响（家务时间减少、消费观念变化）
段 3：*Overall...* 判断 + 建议（能效与可持续）

**骨架 B（矛盾主导）**
段 1：*Air conditioners, the least common of the three in 2014, became the most common by 2023 — overtaking refrigerators, long seen as the essential appliance. The reversal is the most revealing figure in the table.*
段 2：为什么空调反超（气候变暖 + 住房条件改善 + 价格下降）→ 意味着什么（舒适从奢侈变为预期）
段 3：判断 + 建议

**骨架 C（多维并置，若为双图）**
段 1：两图各一句 + 关联句；段 2：分别解读 + 张力；段 3：综合判断。

> **同一组数据，三种骨架导向三个完全不同的立意**：A 讲"生活水平提高"，B 讲"舒适的定义变了"，C 讲"两图之间的关系"。这正是"灵活度"的实际含义——**结构选择会改变你的观点**。

# 第七章 考场决策流程（写作 35 分钟时间轴）

| 时间 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 0–2 min | **审题**：确认题型（图画/图表/文字）、主题词、必答要点 |
| 2–5 min | **定观点**：用中文写一句话"我要说什么"；**选骨架**（A/B/C/D） |
| 5–8 min | **选句位**：按轮换表定 M1–M9 的字母，在草稿上写标记（如 `B-A-C-A-B`） |
| 8–30 min | **写**：段落按骨架走，句子按句位变体走 |
| 30–35 min | **检查**：时态一致性、主谓一致、拼写、**删掉任何"万能句"** |

> **最后 30 秒必做**：通读一遍，凡是"这句话换成任何题目都成立"的句子，**划掉或改成具体的**。这是反模板的最后一关。

# 附录 · 自检清单

写完一篇后逐条核对：

- [ ] 第一段首句是"引入套话"还是直接进入画面/数据？（应是后者）
- [ ] 有没有出现 M1–M2 之外的老套起手（`As is vividly depicted...` / `With the rapid development...`）？
- [ ] 过渡句是否超过 2 个？（超过说明"元话语"过多，删）
- [ ] 每个原因是否都**与本题具体相关**？（能换到别的题 = 不合格）
- [ ] 大词（heritage / harmony / development）是否都有具体内容支撑？
- [ ] 让步转折句是否只用了 1 次？
- [ ] `Only in this way` 类句式是否只出现 1 次？
- [ ] 段落长度是否有长短变化？（三段等长 = 可疑）
- [ ] 个人标记是否自然出现 1–2 次？
- [ ] 最后一段是否**没有出现数字**（图表题）？
- [ ] 全文有没有一句"放之四海皆准"的话？
