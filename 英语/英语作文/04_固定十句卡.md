# 一、大作文（20 分 · 10 句 · 175–195 词）

## 第一段（3 句 · 40–60 词）· 按题型选 1 套

### A. 图画 · 单图

> **句1（总述，倒装开头，避开 As is vividly shown）**
> `Standing in front of 【地点】 is/are 【人物】, 【分词动作】.`

> **句2（细节：动作 + 对话）**
> `One of them, 【动作】, says, "【台词意译】." Meanwhile, the other replies, "【台词意译】."`

> **句3（字幕 / 点题，二选一）**
> `Below the drawing, the caption reads, "【字幕】."`
> 无字幕时：`Simple as the drawing is, it mirrors a thought-provoking phenomenon.`

### B. 图画 · 双图对比（2014 / 2016 / 2017 / 2020）

> **句1** `The two pictures above display two distinct attitudes toward 【话题】.`
> **句2** `In the left picture, 【人物】 is 【动作】. By contrast, the right one captures 【人物】【动作】.`
> **句3** `The sharp contrast reveals that 【寓意】.`

### C. 单图表（2025 表格 / 柱状 / 折线 / 饼图）

> **句1** `The 【table】 above clearly illustrates the changes in 【图表标题核心词】 from 【年】 to 【年】.`
> **句2** `To be specific, 【项1】 surged from 【数】 to 【数】, while 【项2】 climbed steadily from 【数】 to 【数】.`
> **句3** `It is apparent that 【核心结论】.`

### D. 双图表（2026 饼图 + 柱状图）

> **句1** `The two charts above present a clear picture of 【总主题】: the 【图1】 shows 【主题1】, while the 【图2】 lists 【主题2】.`
> **句2** `To be specific, 【数据1】. Notably, 【最高项】 tops the list at 【X%】, followed by 【项2】 at 【Y%】.`
> **句3** `It is apparent that 【核心结论】.`

### E. 图画 + 图表（2024）

> **句1** `Presented before us are a picture and a chart. The image on the left shows 【场景】, where 【人物动作】.`
> **句2** `Meanwhile, the chart on the right indicates that 【对象】 rose from 【数】 to 【数】.`
> **句3** `The two together suggest that 【因果关联】.`

### F. 文字材料（大纲有、未考过，最低优先级）

> **句1** `According to the excerpt, while many people believe 【误区】, the author argues that 【观点】.`
> **句2** `Personally, I agree, as 【主题】 plays a pivotal role in 【领域】.`
> **句3** `This view deserves our serious reflection.`

## 第二段（4 句 · 75–90 词）· 骨架固定，内容换挡

> **句4（主题句）**
> A 个人品质：`The scenario serves as a compelling reminder that 【品质】 is crucial for 【目标】.`
> B 积极趋势：`The 【chart】 reflects a favorable trend: 【趋势】.`
> C 双刃剑 / 消极：`The 【picture】 reveals how 【事物】 is transforming our lives, bringing both benefits and concerns.`

> **句5（论点一）**
> A：`To begin with, embracing 【品质】 not only broadens our horizons but also equips us with 【能力】.`
> B：`First and foremost, with rising incomes and supportive policies, 【主体】 now have easier access to 【事物】.`
> C：`Undoubtedly, 【事物】 plays a vital role in 【作用】.`

> **句6（论点二）**
> A：`On the contrary, those who 【反面做法】 may gain temporary ease but will miss 【机会】.`
> B：`More importantly, this trend benefits both individuals and society: it 【个人好处】, and 【社会好处】.`
> C：`However, we cannot ignore 【顾虑】: 【风险1】, and 【风险2】.`

> **句7（例证，A/B/C 通用，只改槽位）**
> `A case in point is my roommate, who 【具体行动】, which 【结果】.`

**换挡口诀**：定完第一段先问一句「这题是夸、是批、还是两面看」——
夸 → 走 B，批 → 走 C，讲人 → 走 A。

## 第三段（3 句 · 35–45 词）

> **句8（总结）**
> A：`In conclusion, 【品质】 is not merely an option but the cornerstone of personal growth.`
> B：`To sum up, the rise of 【事物】 marks a positive stride toward a better society.`
> C：`In summary, it is imperative to take coordinated steps to 【目标】.`

> **句9（措施，两个主体）**
> `For one thing, 【主体1】 should 【动作】. For another, 【主体2】 ought to 【动作】.`

> **句10（升华）**
> `Only through such concerted efforts can we 【目标】.`

# 二、小作文（10 分 · 8 句 · 100–112 词）

## 书信版

| # | 句子 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Dear 【X】,` | 知道姓用 Mr./Prof.，不知道用 Sir or Madam |
| 2 | `I am writing this email to 【目的】.` | 目的必须用题干原词 |
| 3 | `Regarding 【主题】, there are several key points I would like to share.` | 万能过渡 |
| 4 | `First of all, 【要点1】.` | 是什么 / 为什么 / 怎么办 三选 |
| 5 | `Moreover, 【要点2】.` | |
| 6 | `In addition, 【要点3】.` | 只剩两点就删这句 |
| 7 | `I hope the information above proves helpful to you.` | 不需要回复时用 |
| 8 | `Looking forward to your early reply.` | 需要回复时用；7、8 二选一 |
| — | `Yours sincerely,` / `Li Ming` | 左下 / 右下 |

> **不需要对方回信**（通知、答复问询、信息分享）：第 7 句 + `Should you have any further questions, please feel free to contact me.`

## 通知版（6 句）

    Moreover, 【活动内容 / 职责要求】.
    In addition, all students who 【资格】 are eligible to participate, and 【奖励】.
    We warmly welcome every interested student to join us.
    If you wish to sign up, please email 【邮箱】 no later than 【截止日】.

                                              The Students' Union
